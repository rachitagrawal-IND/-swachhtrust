SwachhTrust is a crowd-verified trust index for public toilets that catches facilities quietly broken behind official "operational" records.
Public toilets often stay listed as "operational" long after they've actually failed no water, no lighting, overflowing bins because official maintenance logs rarely reflect realtime conditions. swachhTrust lets citizens check in on nearby facilities and rate specific things: water, cleanliness, functionality, soap, lighting, accessibility, and waste bins. That crowd data gets checked against the official record, and when reports keep saying "broken" while the log still says "fine," the facility is automatically flagged for priority repair — closing the reporting gap between what's on paper and what's actually there.
